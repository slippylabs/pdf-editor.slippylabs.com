PDF EDITOR & SIGNATURE SIGNER

Sign, annotate, reorder, and merge PDFs entirely in your browser. Nothing you upload ever leaves this tab — no server, no upload endpoint.

Upload a PDF to get started. Everything below (viewing, signing, text, page management) works once at least one PDF is loaded.

Page 0 of 0

Drag a placed signature or text box to move it, drag its bottom-right handle to resize it, and use the × to remove it. Placement is stored relative to the page, so it exports at the exact position shown here.

PNG keeps a transparent background. JPG will show as an opaque rectangle on the page.

Captured signature

Signatures are placed on whichever page the View tab is currently showing — switch pages there first, then come back here.

The text box lands on the page currently shown in the View tab. Double-click a placed text box afterward to edit its wording.

Add another PDF — its pages append to the list below for merging.

Drag a row to reorder it, or use the arrow buttons (drag-and-drop doesn't work reliably on touch devices). Deleting a page also removes any signature/text placed on it.
